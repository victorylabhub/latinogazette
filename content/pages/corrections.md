---
title: Corrections
description: How to report an error and how we correct it.
updated: 2026-10-23
---
We want to get things right. When we make a factual error, we correct it as quickly as possible and note the correction at the end of the article, with the date and a description of what changed.

Minor changes that do not affect the meaning of an article, such as fixing a typo, may be made without a note.

## Report an error

Email [info@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:info@atlasmedianetwork.org) with the article's link and what you believe is wrong. Please include a source if you have one.
