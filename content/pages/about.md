Latino Gazette is a digital news publication about Latino life in the United States. We cover national news, politics and elections, business, communities, culture, opinion and wellbeing for readers across the country.

## Ownership

Latino Gazette is owned and published by **Atlas Media Network, LLC**, 36 Russ Street, 3rd Floor, Hartford, CT 06106, United States. Atlas Media Network also publishes Votante Latino. More about the company is available at [atlasmedianetwork.org](https://www.atlasmedianetwork.org).

## Not affiliated with similar names

Latino Gazette is not affiliated with any other publication, website, organization or social media account with a similar name. Our only website is latinogazette.com.

## How we work

Every article is signed by its author, dated, and links to the sources behind its facts. Read our [editorial standards](/standards/) and [corrections policy](/corrections/).
