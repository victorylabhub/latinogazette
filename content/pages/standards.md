## Accountability

Every article carries the name of the person who wrote it and the date it was published. When an article is substantially updated, we show the date of the update.

## Sourcing

We verify facts with primary sources whenever possible: official records, government data, court documents, direct interviews and original reporting. Articles link to the sources behind their key facts. When we cannot confirm something, we say so or leave it out.

## Elections

Our election coverage points readers to official sources for voting information: [vote.gov](https://vote.gov) and each state's election authority. We do not publish our own vote counts or projections; election-night results link to the official results pages of the states.

## News and opinion

Opinion pieces are clearly labeled and published in the [Opinion](/opinion/) section. They reflect the views of their authors.

## Images

Photographs and illustrations are credited. We do not alter news photographs in ways that change their meaning.

## Independence

Our ownership is disclosed on our [About us](/about/) page. Our journalists do not accept payment or gifts in exchange for coverage.

## Corrections

We correct errors promptly and transparently. See our [corrections policy](/corrections/).
