Latino Gazette is published by Atlas Media Network, LLC, 36 Russ Street, 3rd Floor, Hartford, CT 06106, United States ("we"). This notice explains what information we collect through latinogazette.com.

## What we collect

**Newsletter.** If you sign up for The Gazette Brief, we collect your email address. We use it only to send the newsletter. Sign-ups are stored with our hosting provider, Netlify, Inc. We do not sell your email address or share it for advertising.

**Messages you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it to respond.

**Technical information.** Our website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like most web hosts, Netlify processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser information to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

**Google News tools.** Our pages load a script from Google (Google News Reader Revenue Manager) that connects the site with Google News features. Google may process information such as your IP address and browser data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run third-party advertising scripts on this website.

## Links to other sites

We link to official sources such as vote.gov and state election offices. When you follow a link, the other site's privacy policy applies. The state picker on our voting guide sends you to vote.gov; we do not receive your selection.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email, or by writing to us. You can ask us to access or delete the information we hold about you by emailing [privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org).

## Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the bottom of this page.
